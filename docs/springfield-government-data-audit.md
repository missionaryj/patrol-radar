# Springfield, Illinois — government-original stop-data audit (2026-11-13)

Tracker: [#55](https://github.com/missionaryj/patrol-radar/issues/55). Parent draft: [PR #49](https://github.com/missionaryj/patrol-radar/pull/49). **Speed-first, free-first discovery.** Do not import a repackaged third-party file into Pro unless its specific reuse rights and official lineage have been established.

## Free public government sources located

1. **City of Springfield Police Transparency Dashboard**: https://maps.springfield.il.us/portal/apps/sites/#/police-transparency (city-hosted; launched Aug. 2026). Its backing ArcGIS service is [SPD/SPD_Transparency](https://maps.springfield.il.us/server/rest/services/SPD/SPD_Transparency/FeatureServer). The browsable service lists **ShotSpotter (2), Calls for Service (6), Confirmed Gun Shots (7), Law Enforcement Cases (8), and Use of Force (9)** as feature layers, plus cases/victims/force/calls/IA tables. There is **no explicitly identified traffic-stop feature layer in this service listing**. General incident and calls-for-service records MUST NOT be automatically reclassified as vehicle stops; their geometries and stop-event identities have not been verified. The [Law Enforcement Cases layer](https://maps.springfield.il.us/server/rest/services/SPD/SPD_Transparency/FeatureServer/8) exposes `OccurredIncidentType`, `CommonName`, `BEAT`, and dates, but its metadata does **not** establish vehicle-stop-only events. Do not ingest its point geometry into Stop History without an explicit stop-classification and unique event validation.
2. **Official police-beat boundary service**: [SPD/Beat FeatureServer](https://maps.springfield.il.us/server/rest/services/SPD/Beat/FeatureServer), indexed from the city's [SPD GIS folder](https://maps.springfield.il.us/server/rest/services/SPD) alongside MapServer equivalent. This is a promising government-original geometry source for a future **coarse beat choropleth**. Its polygon metadata/bytes, beat-code attribute, geometry validity, spatial reference, historical effective date, whether features are publicly downloadable, and commercial reuse terms have **not** yet been verified because direct GIS detail/query requests failed in the current reader. Do not equate today's boundaries with historical boundaries for earlier records.
3. **IDOT official annual traffic-stop reports**: https://idot.illinois.gov/form-and-reports/crash-reports/illinois-traffic-and-pedestrian-stop-study.html lists annual Part I/II summaries; agency-level tables are *reporting statistics*, not published street-positioned encounters. A request for statewide 2024–2025 event files was already sent to IDOT, and a second duplicate request was not sent today.
4. **Springfield Police official archived crime statistics**: https://springfield.il.us/Departments/PoliceDepartment/archiveCrimeStats.aspx covers 2017–2025 and provides general crime statistics. These are **not** established as stop events, so are excluded from traffic-stop heat-map imports.
5. **Springfield Police traffic-stop specific statistics**: a city/police announcement dated 2026-03-24 reports a March 17 enforcement detail with 39 stops at Sangamon Avenue / Dirksen Parkway, and 391 year-to-date stops by officers assigned to traffic details. These selective counts are not a complete stop-level dataset and must not be generalized into historical coverage; see https://springfieldherald.news/springfield-police-department-sees-results-from-recent-traffic-detail-p29826-105.htm (local news republication; verify on City site before using as primary-source statistics).

**Beware a false lead:** https://springfield.data.socrata.com/ is a splash page with some category links to `springfield.demo.socrata.com`. This is not verified to be Springfield, Illinois's own motor-vehicle-stop event data; do not import it by name alone.

## Commercial use, no-fee acquisition and constraints

- City Clerk publishes formal [FOIA contact details](https://www.cityclerk.springfield.il.us/Contact.aspx) and [commercial FOIA process](https://www.cityclerk.springfield.il.us/FOIA/FOIADefault.aspx). Commercial requests may take **21 business days**, contradicting the speed-first goal; the [2026 fee schedule](https://cityclerk.springfield.il.us/FOIA/ScheduleFees2026.pdf) lists possible electronic-copy fees. Do **not** commission paid exports, and do not treat an email inquiry as authorizing a billed FOIA request.
- On 2026-11-13, an **informational, zero-fee link-only email was sent** to `city.clerk@springfield.il.us` asking about freely published Springfield traffic-stop CSV/ArcGIS data, valid police-beat boundaries and public reuse terms. Explicitly **no paid FOIA request** or fees authorized. Continue researching independently; do not wait for this email to make progress elsewhere.
- Illinois's state Open Operating Standards Act (20 ILCS 45) is not an automatic blanket licence for municipal City of Springfield data or for news organizations' repackaged releases. Verify applicable source-specific data permissions and commercial reuse rules before enabling a paid Pro layer.
- Current public ArcGIS metadata was identified, but successful full byte downloads / authoritative event CSV and boundary polygon query responses were **not obtained**. Do not claim data acquisition.
