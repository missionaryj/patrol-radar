# City chat: terms of use and content policy

Version 1 · October 30, 2026

City chat is a local feed for people in the same city. By tapping **I agree** you accept these rules. If you break them, your posts can be removed and you can be banned.

## What city chat is for

- Sharing general, public information, like "Flock camera on Neil St is back up" or "lots of Axon hits near campus tonight".
- Talking about patrol-radar and how it works where you are.

## Not allowed

- **Threats or violence** against anyone, including police officers, or encouraging others to hurt, follow, block or interfere with anyone.
- **Doxxing**: names, home addresses, phone numbers, emails, photos, badge numbers or other personal details of any person, including officers.
- **Street addresses and license plates.** Say a general area ("near downtown") instead.
- **Harassment, hate speech** or slurs about any group.
- **Illegal activity**, including help evading a traffic stop or arrest.
- **Spam**, ads, scams or links to malware.
- **Sexual content** or anything involving minors.
- **Impersonating** police, patrol-radar staff or another user.

## How it works

- Your chat room is the city you're in. It's worked out on your phone from the same rough ~150 m map area the app already uses. Your exact location is never sent.
- You're anonymous. You get a random name, like "Teal Fox", that changes every day. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
- You can post 1 message every 30 seconds, and new accounts have a daily limit.
- Messages are deleted after 24 hours. Reported messages are kept for up to 7 days so a moderator can review them, then deleted.
- A message is hidden automatically once several people report it.
- You can report any message or user, and block anyone. You won't see posts from people you block.

## Moderation

- Moderators review reports, remove posts and ban accounts that break these rules, with or without warning.
- Messages that look like threats, addresses, plates or phone numbers are blocked before they're sent.
- We may share information with authorities when the law requires it or when someone's safety is at risk. Because chat is anonymous, there is very little to share: the message, the time, the city room and a random account ID.

## Safety

- Never use the chat while driving.
- Don't rely on the chat for your safety. Posts can be wrong, old or made up.
- In an emergency, call 911.
