# Nationwide traffic-stop source research — 2026-11-12

This is a discovery and validation ledger, not a nationwide coverage claim. `stop-heat-source-inventory.json` covers all 50 states plus DC, with 514 catalog table leads and Stanford archive download links. Many leads describe overlapping years, person tables, or pedestrian stops. No candidate is automatically imported. Eight states had no stop-table candidate in the retrieved catalogs: AL, AK, DE, HI, ME, NM, UT, WV. This does **not** establish that those states lack records.

## Priority statewide sources

- **Stanford OPP**: [downloads](https://openpolicing.stanford.edu/data/) and [location-specific processing notes](https://github.com/stanford-policylab/opp/blob/master/data_readme.md). Researcher-standardized government records, **not** a federal nationwide database. ODC-BY attribution required. The executable registry pins SHA-256 checksums for Montana, Vermont and San Francisco archives. Other archives are discovery candidates only; a `location` column alone is not a street coordinate.
- **California RIPA**: [official data portal](https://openjustice.doj.ca.gov/data), [2024 archive](https://data-openjustice.doj.ca.gov/sites/default/files/dataset/2025-12/ripa-stop-data-2024.zip), [2024 documentation](https://data-openjustice.doj.ca.gov/sites/default/files/dataset/2025-12/ripa-stop-dataset-readme-2024.pdf). Vehicle and pedestrian/person tables require encounter-level joins and an explicit vehicle predicate. Reporting agency presence does not locate a stop. County/agency identifiers do not establish street precision. Current statewide archive is a **discovered download**, not acquired/validated in this change. Do not add it to overlapping municipal/OPP years without an ownership rule.
- **North Carolina**: [state Justice Data Portal](https://www.ncdps.gov/about-dps/boards-and-commissions/governors-crime-commission/criminal-justice-analysis-center/north-carolina-justice-data-portal), [Open Data Policing](https://opendatapolicing.com/), and OPP statewide archive in the inventory. OPP municipal subsets derive from the statewide collection: never sum both. OPP NC does not supply validated street coordinates for this adapter. The `/nc/` research URL returned “Resource not found”; do not present that route as a working downloader. Current collection/freshness must be verified at the state source; earlier conversation claims are not evidence.
- **Virginia**: [official CKAN collection](https://data.virginia.gov/dataset/community-policing-data). Queried the CKAN metadata and confirmed a downloadable [Community Policing CSV](https://data.virginia.gov/dataset/de833a43-7019-444c-8384-9e0cf5255140/resource/60506bbb-685f-4360-8a8c-30e137ce3615/download/community-policing-data.csv). Full file not acquired. Verify motor-vehicle stop categories, local geography codes, reporting completeness and whether usable event coordinates exist before importing. County or town summaries must remain coarse.
- **Connecticut**: [official stop table](https://data.ct.gov/d/nahi-zqrt). Downloaded metadata: town/intersection fields are available; this table has no latitude/longitude columns. Do not invent street points. A future town-polygon adapter can report jurisdiction precision separately from geocoded-stop area. Preserve test-record flags and source intervention identifiers in validation logic.
- **New Jersey**: [official OLEPS page](https://www.njoag.gov/oleps/) and [2020 NJSP dataset](https://data.nj.gov/d/kie7-5sud). Downloaded metadata: year/month, troop/station, outcomes and interaction counts; no event coordinates. OPP notes a CAD/RMS join limitation, although CAD incidents were grouped by incident ID. A station location is not a stop location. Do not explode year/month summaries into fabricated dates.
- **Missouri**: [Attorney General vehicle-stop reports](https://ago.mo.gov/get-help/vehicle-stops-report/). Official reports and an incident-level submission template exist; a template is not released event data. OPP MO is expanded aggregate data: Jan 1 dates are year placeholders, and `location` is agency work location (including Jefferson City for highway patrol). It must not enter date/weekday or geocoded-stop heatmaps as individual located events.
- **Illinois**: [IDOT traffic/pedestrian stop study](https://idot.illinois.gov/form-and-reports/crash-reports/illinois-traffic-and-pedestrian-stop-study.html). Published reports are downloadable; the page directs requests for underlying data to IDOT. No request was sent. OPP statewide beat codes require agency-scoped geometry; they cannot be joined across unrelated cities. Chicago is quarantined because the publisher documents unresolved duplicates; Urbana was previously incorrectly tied to Champaign beats.
- **Oregon**: [official STOP program](https://www.oregon.gov/cjc/stop/Pages/default.aspx) and [Portland stops](https://www.portland.gov/police/open-data/stops-data). OPP archive appears in the inventory. Vehicle/pedestrian separation, event identity and location precision still need validation. Report PDFs/agency totals do not establish event-level street coverage.

## Requested municipal sources

- **San Francisco**: pinned historical OPP file imported. OPP documents raw-field deduplication; this adapter only verifies publisher row identity. It cannot independently prove encounter uniqueness. Later RIPA years must remain partitioned from overlapping publishers.
- **Cincinnati**: [official contact cards](https://data.cincinnati-oh.gov/d/unm9-wj3w). Downloaded projected fields only. Public-domain designation present in metadata. Driver tokens are required; passengers, pedestrians, owner-only and unlisted roles are excluded. Same report IDs with consistent location/date/time merge outcomes. Publisher dates extend back to 1990; the early tail is retained as reported and is **not** independently corroborated. Do not infer continuous reporting from the min/max range. Older `svan-pass`, `ktgf-4sjh` and OPP releases overlap and are excluded.
- **San Diego**: [official quarterly RIPA basic details](https://data.sandiego.gov/datasets/police-ripa-stops/). Beat and block geography needs a compatible beat layer and stop/person joins. The OPP service-area codes are not interchangeable with nine-division polygons. No geocoding or import performed.
- **Los Angeles**: [LAPD incident details](https://data.lacity.org/d/5gp9-8nrb). Metadata acquired: `stop_number` is the incident identifier, reporting district is location geography, and incidents can involve multiple persons. This incident table alone does not provide the vehicle-versus-pedestrian classification needed for the requested filter, nor street coordinates. Join official person/detail tables conservatively before adding district aggregates.
- **Philadelphia**: [official dataset landing page](https://opendataphilly.org/datasets/vehicle-pedestrian-investigations/), [Carto table schema](https://phl.carto.com/api/v2/sql?q=SELECT%20*%20FROM%20car_ped_stops%20LIMIT%200). Metadata acquired. The table offers `stoptype`, date, geometry/point coordinates and record IDs; person-level rows must not automatically become distinct vehicle encounters. A stable encounter grouping and explicit vehicle predicate remain to be verified. No raw person fields acquired.
- **Washington DC**: [MPD stop data explanation](https://mpdc.dc.gov/node/1464816) and [official table](https://maps2.dcgis.dc.gov/dcgis/rest/services/DCGIS_DATA/Public_Safety_WebMercator/MapServer/41). Official metadata acquired. This layer explicitly has **no geometry** and describes one person per row; `STOP_LOCATION_BLOCK` is block text and `STOP_DISTRICT` is coarse geography. The catalog's geospatial categorization does not prove stop coordinates exist. Do not map officer assignment districts as stop locations or count people/tickets as encounters.
- **Nashville**: [official vehicle-stop map](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/police/data-dashboard/vehicle-stops-map), [official reports](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/police/executive-services/strategic-development/crime-analysis/reports), historical OPP download in inventory. OPP describes raw-field deduplication and published coordinates, making a historical adapter feasible; not acquired/imported in this change. The official map page's update date does not establish current event availability.

## St. Louis County and City

[County Police records page](https://stlouiscountypolice.com/resources-services/records-and-permits/) points to the [County NextRequest portal](https://recordsrequest.stlouiscountymo.gov/). Its public landing page exposes a Search link and says records may already be available. The search results route did not render in the text reader; a subsequent browser attempt was denied permission. **The archive was not searched to completion.** No conclusion that event-level stops do not exist is justified.

The [City Sunshine instructions](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/government/departments/counselor/sunshine-requests.cfm) direct users to GOV/QA's Public Records Archive, including SLMPD records. The [City portal](https://stlouismo.govqa.us/WEBAPP/_rs/SupportHome.aspx) did not expose searchable records through the text reader. [City traffic-stop equity archives](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/archives/resilience/equity/justice/policing/traffic-stops.cfm) and [CPE study findings](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/government/departments/public-safety/police/national-justice-database-study.cfm) establish analysis of stops, not a downloadable validated event-location file. Searches and released-file inspection remain open work; no records request or message was submitted.

Police social posts, incident feeds, mugshots, calls for service, crash reports and unverified sightings have contributed **zero records**. They may point to an official release but cannot substitute for confirmed traffic-stop records. Residential driver ZIP codes, officer work locations, jurisdiction centroids and guessed intersections are never stop coordinates.

## Remaining acquisition work

For the eight catalog gaps, the additional primary-source checks found:

- **AL**: [ALEA annual reports](https://www.alea.gov/annual-reports) and enforcement summaries, not a validated downloadable event-location table.
- **AK**: [DPS daily dispatch](https://dailydispatch.dps.alaska.gov/) includes selected incident narratives explicitly describing some stops. These are selective reports and contain identifying details; they were not scraped or imported. They cannot establish complete traffic-stop coverage.
- **DE**: [Delaware State Police reports](https://dsp.delaware.gov/reports/) publish traffic statistics; [University of Delaware open data](https://www.udel.edu/police/crime-stats/open-data/) links a traffic-warning/arrest summary CSV. Neither is established here as geolocated encounter records.
- **HI**: [Honolulu reports](https://www.honolulupd.org/information/annual-report/) and the [information portal](https://www.honolulupd.org/information/) expose statistics, arrest logs and calls for service. Those other record types were explicitly excluded; no verified stop-event download was acquired.
- **ME**: [Maine AG profiling/data-collection reports](https://www.maine.gov/ag/about-us/special-projects/racial-profiling) document a new statewide collection and reporting limitations. An empty OPD/OPP inventory entry does not mean Maine has no stop data. No usable event-coordinate release was acquired.
- **NM**: [NMDOT Traffic Records](https://www.dot.nm.gov/traffic-records-system/) describes TraCS collection of citations/crashes; a software/data-collection system is not evidence of a public stop-level download.
- **UT**: [Utah highway safety traffic-records plan](https://highwaysafety.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/22/2025/06/UT_FY26_Traffic-Records-Strategic-Plan.pdf) is a systems/planning lead, not an acquired encounter dataset. No usable official download was verified in this research pass.
- **WV**: [WVSP publications](https://www.wvsp.gov/about/Pages/Publications.aspx) provide annual reports; [Traffic Safety](https://www.wvsp.gov/departments/trafficsafety/Pages/default.aspx) references crash/traffic-record services. Summary counts and crash reports were not imported as stops.

The 51-entry inventory is a starting point, not completed direct validation of 51 publishers. Prioritize documented event identity and coordinates (e.g. Nashville and Philadelphia), then compatible coarse jurisdiction/beat adapters for statewide datasets. Before adding any source: verify official provenance and reuse terms, identify the unit of observation, publish a vehicle filter, establish a non-overlapping publisher/year/agency partition, acquire a stable snapshot, test coordinate/date checks, and reconcile all rows. No 75% state, population or geographic-area goal has been attained.
