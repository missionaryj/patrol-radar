Help

Not seeing police on the map?

Sometimes police gear doesn't show up. A short recording helps fix that. Here's how to send one:

  1. Scan. Use the Android app if you can, and set Android app scan mode to Fastest (Settings → Scanning). Tap Scan.
  2. Record. When you're near police, pull up the Live detections panel and tap ● Record.
  3. Keep recording a few minutes while you stay nearby (only when it's safe).
  4. Tap ■ Stop rec, then tap Export. This saves a patrol-radar-session-….json file.
  5. Tap Email the debug file below, attach the exported file, and add a short note: what you saw and roughly when.
✉️ Email the debug file Sends to percolatorbiz@gmail.com from your own email app. The file can't be attached automatically. Attach it yourself. What's in the file: only police-gear matches (other devices are dropped), device IDs that are scrambled and change every session, ~150 m map areas (never your exact location), signal strength and times, the matched signal data with serial-like text masked, and scan-health counts. No MAC addresses, device names or Wi-Fi network names.

Privacy

  • Receive-only: never connects, pairs or writes to a device.
  • Device IDs are salted hashes that change every session.
  • Saved locations are ~200 m areas; your exact spot stays on screen only.
