Patrol Radar – Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026. Applies to the Patrol Radar Android app (io.github.missionaryj.patrolradar) and the web app at missionaryj.github.io/patrol-radar.

Patrol Radar is a public-awareness tool. It listens passively for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisements that nearby equipment already broadcasts in public and shows likely public-safety equipment on a coarse map. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

Bluetooth

  • Scanning is passive only. The app never connects to, pairs with, or writes to any device.
  • Device identifiers are replaced with a salted hash. The salt is random and changes every session, so hashes cannot be linked across sessions. MAC addresses and serial numbers are not stored; serial-like text in device names is masked.
  • Phones, headphones and other consumer devices are hidden from the map by default.

Location

  • Your precise location is used only to draw your position on screen. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Detections are snapped to a coarse grid of about 150–250 m before they are stored or shared.
  • On Android, a foreground service with a visible notification keeps scanning while the screen is off. It runs only after you tap Start and stops when you tap Stop. The app does not request background (“all the time”) location.

Data stored on your device

  • Recent detections (coarse cell, category, time) are kept in app storage and expire after 15 minutes.
  • If you use the session recorder, matched police-gear records (coarse cell, hashed ID, category, signal strength, time) are kept on your device, capped at 5,000 entries, until you clear them. Exporting a session file happens only when you choose to, and you control where it goes.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Shared crowd map (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sharing, each report sent to patrol-radar-api.vercel.app contains only: the coarse grid cell, a 5-minute time slot, the gear category, a confidence level, and a random session ID that rotates and resets when you turn sharing off. No precise location, device identifiers, names or account data are sent.

  • The server keeps a hash of your IP address, salted with a value that changes every hour, only to enforce rate limits. The raw IP address is not stored.
  • Reports expire after about 2 hours. Public map reads are delayed by 12–17 minutes.
  • Data is hosted on Vercel and Upstash Redis.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), which receives your IP address and the map area being viewed, as with any web map. See the OSMF privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Selling or sharing

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Clear the session in the app or uninstall it to delete everything stored on your device. Shared crowd reports expire automatically in about 2 hours and cannot be linked back to you.
