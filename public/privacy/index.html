Patrol Radar – Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Applies to the Patrol Radar Android app (io.github.missionaryj.patrolradar) and the web app at missionaryj.github.io/patrol-radar.

Patrol Radar is a public-awareness tool. It listens passively for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisements that nearby equipment already broadcasts in public and shows likely public-safety equipment on a coarse map. There are no accounts (except the optional anonymous chat account described below), no ads, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

Bluetooth

  • Scanning is passive only. The app never connects to, pairs with, or writes to any device.
  • Device identifiers are replaced with a salted hash. The salt is random and changes every session, so hashes cannot be linked across sessions. MAC addresses and serial numbers are not stored; serial-like text in device names is masked.
  • Phones, headphones and other consumer devices are hidden from the map by default.

Location

  • Your precise location is used only to draw your position on screen. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Detections are snapped to a coarse grid of about 150–250 m before they are stored or shared.
  • On Android, a foreground service with a visible notification keeps scanning while the screen is off. It runs only after you tap Start and stops when you tap Stop. The app does not request background (“all the time”) location.

Data stored on your device

  • Recent detections (coarse cell, category, time) are kept in app storage and expire after 15 minutes.
  • If you use the session recorder, matched police-gear records (coarse cell, hashed ID, category, signal strength, time) are kept on your device, capped at 5,000 entries, until you clear them. Exporting a session file happens only when you choose to, and you control where it goes.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Shared crowd map (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sharing, each report sent to patrol-radar-api.vercel.app contains only: the coarse grid cell, a 5-minute time slot, the gear category, a confidence level, and a random session ID that rotates and resets when you turn sharing off. No precise location, device identifiers, names or account data are sent.

  • The server keeps a hash of your IP address, salted with a value that changes every hour, only to enforce rate limits. The raw IP address is not stored.
  • Reports expire after about 2 hours. Public map reads are delayed by 12–17 minutes.
  • Data is hosted on Vercel and Upstash Redis.

City chat (optional)

  • City chat is off until you open it and agree to the chat rules. If you never open it, nothing in this section applies to you.
  • Opening chat creates an anonymous account with a random ID. No email, phone number, name or password is collected. Your display name is a random handle generated by the server that changes every day.
  • Your chat room is the nearest city, worked out on your phone from the coarse ~150–250 m map area using an offline list of towns. Only the room name (for example “US-IL-champaign”) is sent. Your location, the map cell, device identifiers and scan data are never sent with chat.
  • We store the messages you post (text, time, city room, daily handle and your anonymous ID), your acceptance of the chat rules, reports you make, and the users you block. This is needed to show the feed, enforce the 1-message-per-10-seconds limit and daily caps, and act on reports.
  • Messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours. Messages that were reported are kept for up to 7 days so a moderator can review them, then deleted. You can delete your own messages at any time with the Delete button next to them.
  • Messages are public to anyone in the same city room. Do not post personal information. Automatic filters block phone numbers, street addresses, license plates, email addresses and threats, and moderators may remove posts and ban accounts that break the rules.
  • Chat is hosted on Supabase (supabase.com) and sent over HTTPS. Supabase processes the data on our behalf and, like any web service, sees your IP address when you connect.
  • We may share chat information with authorities when the law requires it or when someone’s safety is at risk. Because chat is anonymous, this is limited to the message, its time, the city room and the random account ID.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), which receives your IP address and the map area being viewed, as with any web map. See the OSMF privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Selling or sharing

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Clear the session in the app or uninstall it to delete everything stored on your device. Shared crowd reports expire automatically in about 2 hours and cannot be linked back to you. Chat messages delete themselves after 24 hours (reported ones within 7 days), and you can delete your own messages sooner. To have your anonymous chat account and everything linked to it removed, open an issue at the contact link below or email percolatorbiz@gmail.com with the time and city room of one of your messages.
